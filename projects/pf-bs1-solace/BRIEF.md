# BRIEF — BS1 · Brand System in Motion

**Brand:** Solace — a wellness & sleep brand (fictional)  
**Length / format:** 18 s, 16:9

## Concept
A living brand book: logo construction → palette → type → pattern → applications.

## Look (palette, type, finish)
Elegant: sand #efe6da, terracotta #c8643b, sage #8aa58c, deep brown ink #2b211b. Instrument Serif (display, italics for accents) + Manrope (labels). Generous whitespace, slow precise motion, layered paper-like panels with soft shadows.

## Beats (fit the timing to the music's strong beats / drops; every text stays up long enough to read)
- 0–3.5 logo construction: a crescent + circle mark draws with thin construction guides that then fade; wordmark 'Solace'
- 3.5–7 palette: three swatches rise with names + hex codes (Terracotta #C8643B …)
- 7–10 type specimen: a huge 'Aa' + a pangram + scale steps
- 10–13.5 pattern: the mark tiles into a slowly rotating pattern
- 13.5–18 applications bento: app icon, sleep-mask label, phone lock screen, tote (flat shapes); lockup 'Solace — rest, designed.' + 'Concept work'

## Craft notes
Slow confident eases ('settle' 0.9–1.2 s), minimal bounce; construction lines with DrawSVG; swatches on one grid; bento panels sized by content.

## Sound
elegant-premium; very light SFX: page-turn (.18) between sections, sparkle (.15) when the logo completes, chime at the end.
