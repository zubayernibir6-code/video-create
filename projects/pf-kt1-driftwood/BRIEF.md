# BRIEF — KT1 · Kinetic Type Manifesto

**Brand:** Driftwood — a calm productivity app (fictional)  
**Length / format:** 20 s, 16:9

## Concept
Manifesto: 'WORK / IS LOUD. / NOTIFICATIONS. / MEETINGS. / NOISE. / WHAT IF / IT WASN'T? / MAKE WORK / FEEL LIGHTER. / Driftwood.'

## Look (palette, type, finish)
Colour-block kinetic type: cobalt #2340ff, cream #f3eadb, ink #111. Anton (uppercase, 200–400 px) for slams + Inter 500 for small text. Big type may bleed off the frame edges on purpose; colour fields wipe on the beat.

## Beats (fit the timing to the music's strong beats / drops; every text stays up long enough to read)
- each line on a strong beat of social-punch (read its .beats.json): slams, splits, word swaps, a line that shakes then calms
- mood switch at 'WHAT IF / IT WASN'T?': fast cuts (0.5 s) → slow breathing type (1.5 s) — duck the music there
- end: 'Driftwood' wordmark settling + 'Make work feel lighter.' + 'Concept work'

## Craft notes
Every slam on a strong beat. Hard cuts in the loud part, soft dissolves in the calm part. Vary entrances (scale-down slam, mask-up, char cascade, split from centre, slide with snap). ≤ 3 flashes per second.

## Sound
social-punch; whoosh on slams, impact-bass-1 short on the heaviest three, music ducked to .4 on the calm line, sparkle on the wordmark.
