# Asset inventory

**No assets were captured.** This project runs in no-capture mode: the product has no
website, no screenshots and no logo (see `BRIEF.md` → `## Assets`). The user supplied no
asset notes.

Every visual must be **invented in HTML** from the brief — Bengali typography, numerals,
colour fields, and abstract shapes that *suggest* an interface. Do not fabricate a
screenshot of a product that does not exist.

  Playfair Display, JetBrains Mono.
