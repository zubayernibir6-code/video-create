# R2 — Finding $1,000–$5,000 clients, getting paid in Bangladesh, removing blockers

Prepared 2026-11-07 for a one-person AI-assisted motion-graphics studio (Mirzapur, Tangail; owner has a day job as an accountant).
Method: about 35 web searches and 20 page fetches on 2026-11-07. Many official pages (Upwork, Fiverr help, Payoneer pricing) returned 403/404 to the fetch tool, so several fee numbers come from secondary blogs. Every such number is flagged **[UNVERIFIED]**. Items marked **[LEGAL RISK]** need a local lawyer or chartered accountant before acting. I am not a lawyer or tax adviser.

---

## 0. Executive summary (read this first)

1. **Best expected-value path for a newcomer without reviews: direct outbound to recently launched or funded SaaS/AI startups, led by a spec teaser, plus LinkedIn/X visibility.** Marketplaces (Upwork, Contra) are the secondary path and give the first reviews. Toptal and Fiverr Pro are long shots until there is a track record.
2. **Cleanest payment setup today:** client pays by wire/ACH/card invoice or through Upwork. Money lands in **Payoneer** (USD receiving account), then withdraws to a **Bangladeshi bank account in BDT** (or to bKash via Payoneer). Keep every invoice, contract and platform statement. Bangladesh Bank's circular of 22 July 2026 now accepts digital evidence (platform statements, emails, invoices, contracts) as proof of foreign income.
3. **Wise cannot be your receiving account from Bangladesh.** Wise's own help page says BDT cannot be added to a Wise account by bank transfer, and secondary sources say residents cannot open a full Wise account (see section 3).
4. **PayPal is not truly available** to Bangladeshi residents for receiving business payments. The only PayPal product that launched was Xoom, in 2017, and that is for incoming remittances from US PayPal holders only. Do not build the business on PayPal.
5. **Stripe does not support Bangladesh** (Stripe's global page, 2026-11-07). A Stripe Atlas Delaware LLC costs $500 plus $100 a year for the agent, plus Form 5472 compliance (about $300–$700 a year), plus Delaware tax. It is not needed to start, and a Bangladeshi resident owning a foreign company may need Bangladesh Bank permission **[LEGAL RISK, not verified by this research]**. Skip it in the first 90 days.
6. **Incentives and tax (the picture is moving):**
   - The 2.5% remittance incentive is for wage-earner remittances, not freelancers.
   - A 4% cash incentive for IT/ITES exports (introduced in a January 2022 circular) is reported for freelancers on listed marketplaces. Its 2026 status is **[UNCONFIRMED]**.
   - A 7.5% source-tax deduction by banks on freelancer inflows was announced as dropped in June 2026. The FY2026-27 budget (presented 11 June 2026) proposed full income-tax and VAT exemption for all freelancers and content creators. I could **not** confirm the Finance Bill was passed with these terms.
7. **Crypto: do not use.** Bangladesh Bank says it does not recognise crypto and has warned that transactions fall under foreign-exchange and anti-money-laundering laws. **Flag as illegal-risk.**
8. **First price:** the first 2–3 projects at $750–$1,200 (30–45 s launch video), in exchange for a named testimonial, a case study and a logo-use right. Then $1,500–$2,500 for the standard package, and $3,000–$5,000 for a "Pro" package after about 3 proofs. Market data says SaaS explainers commonly sit at $1,500–$7,000, so $1,000–$5,000 is in range.
9. **Honesty check on the goal:** reaching consistent $1,000–$5,000 clients inside 30 days with no reviews is possible but not typical. A realistic 30-day target is 1–3 paid projects ($750–$3,000 total) and a pipeline of 5–10 live conversations. Plan for months 2–3 to reach the $2,000+ price level.

### 2.2 The "free spec teaser" tactic

- **What:** make a 10–15 s teaser animating one real screen or one real feature of the prospect's product, then send it with a short note and a fixed price for the full video.
- **Evidence it works:** I found **no rigorous study** for video production services. Evidence is anecdotal and vendor-sourced:
  - A creative-agency outreach page reports 3.8% replies against a 0.74% all-industry median **[UNVERIFIED, vendor]** ([Smartlead](https://www.smartlead.ai/email-templates/creative-agencies-cold-email)).
  - One 2026 vendor playbook describes a 12-person video agency, 90 days, 3-touch sequence, 6.1% positive replies and 8 booked calls (single anecdote) ([Ciela](https://ciela.ai/blogs/how-to-get-ai-agency-clients-with-personalized-video)).
  - Claims that video lifts cold-email replies range from about 20% to 3×. A more sceptical source says it mostly helps in follow-ups and for named accounts worth over $20,000 ([Puzzleinbox, 2026](https://puzzleinbox.com/blog/video-in-cold-email-prospecting-2026)).
- **Risks:**
  1. Unpaid time: budget at most 45–60 minutes of AI-assisted effort per teaser (your pipeline makes this cheap).
  2. IP risk: you are using their logo, UI and brand. Label it "concept, not for use", watermark it, and do not publish it publicly without their permission.
  3. Idea theft: the prospect could use it without paying. Mitigate with a watermark, a low-resolution file, and a link that expires.
  4. Do not do this for every prospect. Do it for the top 20% that fit your best style.
- **My recommendation:** teaser only after a first positive signal (a reply, a follow-back, or a launch date you can point to), except for 10 hand-picked "dream" accounts a week.

### 2.4 Templates (adapt in your own voice; keep under 100 words)

**Cold email 1 (before teaser)**
> Subject: A 30-second launch video for {Product}?
> Hi {Name} — I saw {Product} is launching {date/just raised}. Your homepage has no demo video; I made a 12-second concept of your {feature} so you can see what I mean (link, watermarked). I make 30–45 s SaaS launch videos in 7–10 days for $1,200 fixed. Want the full version for {launch date}? — {You}, {portfolio link}

**LinkedIn connection note (≤200 characters)**
> Hi {Name}, congrats on the {launch/round}. I make short launch videos for SaaS teams — made a 12 s concept of {feature}, happy to share if useful.

**Follow-up 1 (day 3)**
> Quick nudge, {Name}. Here is a version with captions for LinkedIn/X. If a video is on the launch list, I have 2 slots left this month.

**Break-up (day 17)**
> I will stop here so I do not clutter your inbox. If a launch video comes up later, the concept link stays live for 30 days. Good luck with {launch}.

### 2.5 Legal notes on cold email (informational, not advice)

- **CAN-SPAM (US):** applies to B2B email too; no prior consent is required, but you must include a real sender identity, a physical postal address, an honest subject line, and honour opt-outs promptly (sources say within 10 business days) ([Instantly](https://instantly.ai/blog/b2b-email-list-compliance-gdpr-canspam); [Mailshake](https://mailshake.com/?p=18047)). Penalty amounts per email are quoted between about $43,000 and $53,000 and change yearly; confirm on FTC.gov.
- **GDPR (EU/UK targets):** B2B cold email can rely on "legitimate interest" (Art. 6(1)(f)), but you need a relevant business reason, an easy opt-out, and to stop on request. Safer: target US startups first and avoid EU/UK individuals' personal emails.
- **Practical rules:** use a business-domain email (not Gmail) with SPF/DKIM, send low volume, include an unsubscribe line ("reply STOP"), and keep a do-not-contact list.

### 3.5 Recommended cleanest setup

1. Get a **Bangladesh Freelancer ID** (free).
2. Open **Payoneer** (individual, USD receiving account) in your own name and link a **personal BD bank account** (choose a bank that is known to handle freelancer remittances well and ask them if they deduct source tax; Dutch-Bangla Bank is reported to have suspended it).
3. Optionally link **bKash** inside the bKash app for small, quick withdrawals.
4. Invoice clients from a template with your legal name and address, a clear description ("motion graphics video production services") and a Payoneer receiving-account link (clients can pay by ACH or card via Payoneer's request-payment feature), or ask the client to use Upwork or Contra escrow for the first project.
5. For clients who insist on PayPal, Wise, Stripe or Venmo: decline politely, or accept Upwork/Contra as an intermediary. Do **not** accept crypto.
6. Keep a folder per client: contract, invoice, platform statement, and the bank advice. Under the July 2026 circular this is the proof the bank will ask for.
7. Review in 90 days: if revenue passes about $2,000 a month, speak to a CA about registration and, only then, about a US company.

## 5. Communication blockers (non-native English, AI-assisted)

| Blocker | Fix | Notes |
|---|---|---|
| Written English quality | Draft with Claude, then read aloud once; keep sentences under 20 words; use a saved snippets file for 10 repeated messages (greeting, quote, delay notice, revision request, invoice, delivery, thank-you, scope change, late payment, referral ask) | Clients judge clarity, not accent |

| Discovery calls | Offer a 15-minute call with a written agenda; or do it **asynchronously** by sending 5 questions and a Loom reply. Ask: goal, audience, where it will run (website, PH, ads), length, assets they have, deadline, budget range, who approves | Script below |
| Async video (Loom) | Use a short Loom to walk through the quote and later the animatic. I found no pricing in the searches; check Loom's plan page (its free plan has had a video-length and count limit) **[UNVERIFIED]**. Free alternatives: OBS to YouTube unlisted | — |
| Time zones | Dhaka is UTC+6. US East is 10–11 hours behind, US West 13–14. Offer two daily windows: Dhaka 08:00–10:00 (US evening) and 21:00–23:00 (US morning). Everything else is async. Publish these in your proposal and email signature | Standard conversion |
| Response time | Marketplace and startup buyers expect same-day replies; aim to reply in under 4 working hours in US time and say "I will reply within 24 h" on your page. If you cannot reply, send an auto-reply with a time. No source quantified this; it is my recommendation | **[UNVERIFIED]** |
| Day job | Block two fixed daily slots (for example 07:00–08:30 and 21:30–23:00 Dhaka) for client work; tell clients the real turnaround ("7 business days from approved script") rather than a promise you cannot keep | — |

**Discovery-call script (15 min)**
1. "Thanks for the time. My goal is to leave with a clear scope and a fixed quote."
2. What is the video for, and where will it run?
3. Who is the viewer, and what should they do after watching?
4. Length and format (16:9, 9:16, 1:1)? Voice-over language?
5. What assets exist (logo, UI recordings, brand colours, script)?
6. When do you need it live? Who approves?
7. Budget range? ("Most of my launch-video clients invest $1,500–$3,000. Does that fit?")
8. Recap and next steps; send the quote within 24 h.

### 6.2 How fast to raise

- Rule: raise 25–30% after every 2 completed projects, or immediately if you win 3 of your next 5 pitches (price too low).
- Remove the discount only in exchange for: named testimonial, a case study, a logo-use right and one referral intro.
- Never quote "starting from" without a number; give the fixed price per package.
- Local note: a $750 first price equals about Tk 90,000 at roughly Tk 120 per USD **[exchange rate approximate]**, which is competitive with the local market below.

## 9. 30-day launch plan

Assumptions: 8–10 hours a week of your own time, Claude/agents do the production.

### Week 1 (Nov 8–14): foundation
- Open **Payoneer** (individual), link one BD bank account, apply for the **Freelancer ID** (needs $50 verified income in 12 months; if you do not have it yet, apply after the first payment).
- Book one paid consult with a CA or tax lawyer: freelance income category, TIN, whether a trade licence is needed, whether the 4% incentive can apply.
- Check your employment contract for outside work.
- Build the **portfolio page**: 45–60 s reel, 15 s cut, 30 s SaaS cut, 4 projects, 3 package cards (prices from section 6), "Book a call" button, time zone note.
- Create 3 labelled **concept case studies** of public products (target: 3 × 30 s).
- Write the contract and invoice templates (one page each). Create the snippet bank (10 messages).
- Create a **Product Hunt** and **Upwork** profile; on Upwork choose one specialised profile "SaaS launch and product explainer videos" and add a 60 s intro video.
- **Numbers:** 3 case studies, 1 reel, 1 portfolio page, 1 Payoneer account.

### Week 2 (Nov 15–21): start outbound and marketplace
- Build a list of **60 prospects**: 30 Product Hunt upcoming/launching-soon makers, 20 recently funded startups (last 30 days), 10 YC batch companies. Record name, role, product, trigger, and a hook sentence.
- Send **25–30 contacts** (email plus LinkedIn note), with 5 spec teasers to the top 5 accounts.
- Post **3 clips** on LinkedIn and X (a before/after, a process clip, a reel).
- Upwork: send **8–10 proposals** to jobs matching your profile (about 16–20 Connects each at typical rates, so under $5 per proposal **[UNVERIFIED]**); apply to Contra; list 2 services (Teaser and Launch Video).
- **Numbers:** 30 outreach contacts, 5 teasers, 10 proposals, 3 social posts, goal 2–3 replies.

### Week 3 (Nov 22–28): follow up, convert, and add local
- Follow-ups 1 and 2 to week-2 contacts (day 3–4 and day 9–10), plus a fresh batch of **30 new contacts** with 5 teasers.
- Run **discovery calls** with replies. Send fixed quotes within 24 h with the Launch price package; require the 30–50% deposit before work.
- **Local push:** message 10 warm local contacts and 10 local brands (from the Facebook Ad Library) with the "Ad Pack" offer, using Bengali examples.
- Post a 4th and 5th clip; join 2–3 communities (a motion design Slack/Discord) and answer questions without selling.
- **Numbers:** 30 new contacts, 5 teasers, 3–5 calls, 2 quotes sent, 1–2 local conversations, goal: 1 signed project (cash in, even $500–$1,200).

### Week 4 (Nov 29–Dec 6): deliver, collect proof, raise the price
- Deliver the first project in 7–10 days; ask for a testimonial, a logo-use right and one referral the day you deliver; publish the case study within 7 days.
- Third outreach batch (30 contacts) using the first project as proof, now with the standard-price list for new quotes.
- Convert any Upwork wins; if you have 3+ jobs, ask for reviews.
- Apply to **Fiverr Pro** only after 5–10 orders, so skip for now; review Toptal in 12 months.
- Review the numbers: contacts sent, replies, calls, quotes, wins. If replies are under 2%, change the hook and the target list; if replies come but not wins, lower the barrier (offer the Teaser package at $750).
- **Numbers:** 30 new contacts, 1 delivered project, 1 testimonial, 1 case study, pipeline of 5+ live conversations. Realistic revenue target: $750–$3,000 (1–3 projects), with $1,000 as a healthy first-month goal.
- Raise prices by 25% for new quotes once 2 projects are delivered.

### Metrics to track weekly (spreadsheet)
Contacts sent, teasers sent, replies, calls, quotes, wins, revenue, days to deliver, hours spent. A healthy first month: 120 contacts, 15 teasers, 4–6 replies, 3–4 calls, 1–2 wins.
