# Outreach kit (English) — adapt every message to the person; keep each under 100 words

Rules (from docs/business/research/R2-clients-payments-bd.md):
- Contact only people with a real trigger: launching soon (Product Hunt "coming soon"), raised a round in the last
  30 days (TechCrunch / Crunchbase), new YC batch, or a homepage with no product video.
- One clear hook about THEIR product. A link to your portfolio. A fixed price. No attachments in the first message.
- Spec teasers (10–15 s concepts of their product) only for the top 5 prospects a week; watermark "concept", never
  publish them, delete them if asked.
- Cold email law: real name and address in the signature, an easy opt-out line, honest subject (US CAN-SPAM);
  low volume and a relevant reason for EU contacts (GDPR legitimate interest).
- Claude drafts; **Talha reviews and sends** every message himself.

## 1. Cold email (with a teaser)
Subject: A 30-second launch video for {Product}?

Hi {Name} — congrats on {the launch / your round}. Your homepage explains {Product} well, but there is no demo video yet,
so I made a 12-second concept of {feature} to show what I mean: {link, watermarked}.
I make 30–45 s SaaS launch videos in 7–10 days, fixed price $1,200, all formats and captions included.
Want the full version ready for {date}?
— Talha Zubayer, Motion Design · {portfolio link}
(Not relevant? Reply "no" and I won't write again.)

## 2. Cold email (no teaser)
Subject: {Product} — launch video idea

Hi {Name}, I watched your {Product Hunt page / launch post}. One idea: a 30 s film that opens on {their strongest
benefit}, shows {the key screen} in motion and ends on {CTA}. Examples of my work: {portfolio link}. Fixed price,
7–10 days, 16:9 + 9:16 + captions included. Worth a 10-minute chat?
— Talha

## 3. LinkedIn connection note (≤ 200 characters)
Hi {Name}, congrats on {the launch}. I make short launch videos for SaaS teams — made a 12 s concept of {feature},
happy to share if useful.

## 4. Follow-up (day 3–4)
Quick nudge, {Name} — here is the same concept cut for LinkedIn and X (with captions). If a video is on the launch
list, I have 2 slots left this month.

## 5. Follow-up (day 9–10)
One more idea for {Product}: {a different angle, e.g. a 15 s feature drop for your changelog}. Happy to send a
quote — it takes me 24 hours.

## 6. Break-up (day 17)
I'll stop here so I don't clutter your inbox. If a launch video comes up later, the concept link stays live for
30 days. Good luck with {launch}!

## 7. Reply to "how much?"
Thanks, {Name}! For {their need} I'd suggest the {package} — {what is included}. Fixed price ${price}, first draft in
{n} working days, 2 revision rounds, all formats + captions included. 50 % to start, 50 % on delivery
(Payoneer invoice: card or bank transfer). Shall I send the plan and shot table?
